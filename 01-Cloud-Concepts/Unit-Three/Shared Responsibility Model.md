# 🤝 The Shared Responsibility Model

The Shared Responsibility Model identifies which security tasks are handled by the cloud provider and which are handled by the customer.

## ⚖️ The Balance of Responsibility

### 👤 Always the Customer (YOU)
- **Data & Information:** Protecting the integrity and privacy of your data.
- **Devices (Endpoints):** Securing the hardware used to access cloud services.
- **Accounts & Identities:** Managing users, groups, and access permissions.

### ☁️ Always the Provider (Microsoft)
- **Physical Hosts:** The actual servers in the data center.
- **Physical Network:** The cables, routers, and switches.
- **Physical Data Center:** Security guards, power, and climate control.

## 🔄 The "Shifting" Responsibilities
Responsibility for the following items depends on the service model (IaaS, PaaS, or SaaS):
- Operating Systems
- Network Controls
- Applications
- Identity & Infrastructure

> **Rule of Thumb:** As you move from **IaaS ➔ PaaS ➔ SaaS**, the cloud provider takes on **more** responsibility, and the customer takes on **less**.
